## Who We Are

**ByteSphere Solutions Pvt. Ltd.** is a premier technology partner that helps global companies and growing software organizations scale dedicated engineering divisions rapidly, without the operational, legal, and financial friction of establishing local offices.

Rather than acting as a traditional transactional outsourcing agency, Bytesphere structures **long-term, cohesive engineering capability** that integrates natively into our clients' roadmaps, workflows, and culture.

---

## What We Do

### 1. Dedicated Engineering Divisions (BOT Model)
We build, recruit, host, and manage dedicated software engineering teams in Nepal tailored exclusively to your tech stack and roadmap.
- **Build:** We absorb the cost of sourcing, vetting, and hiring top tech talent.
- **Operate:** We handle local payroll, benefits, high-speed equipment, workspace operations, and labor law compliance.
- **Transfer:** Option to legally transfer team members and operational entities directly under your corporate umbrella.

### 2. Enterprise Software Engineering & Delivery
We engineer scalable digital systems, custom business platforms, and enterprise solutions across the entire software lifecycle:
- **Cloud & Microservices Platforms**
- **Operations, CMS & Analytics Hubs**
- **CRM, Finance & Automated Payroll Systems**
- **Interactive Community & Real-Time Support Platforms**
- **Bot & Workflow Process Automation Frameworks**
